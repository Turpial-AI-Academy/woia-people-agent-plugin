# Generic People contract


Core 0.5.7 is the only hard plugin requirement. Workforce, Identity, Communications (internal only), restricted Documents and approved Organization Knowledge resolve via organization bindings and provider qualification. Technical availability grants no business authority.

Every contribution preserves correlation, owning authority/evidence, exact scope, source/revision and allowed fields. Accepted decision and relevant effective period are required for joining/role-change/leaving; operational owners retain commitments. Filter before retrieval and response. Missing/stale/conflicting source means UNKNOWN and referral to its owner, never invented policy. Guidance identifies current approved procedure version.

Competence, authority, requested access, technically applied access and readiness remain distinct. Technology executes accepted access changes and observed revocation. Current revoked grants override historical applied snapshots. Coverage requires competent preparation, exact current authority/period, owning department acceptance and independent review preservation; proposal alone cannot fill a gap. Departure preserves identity/history and other relationships.

External staff/nonstaff roles always use Customer Service for allowed contact. No hiring/dismissal, autonomous recruiting, salary/leave policy, financial effects or access issue are implemented. Sender cannot edit receiver Tasks or inherit receiver authority. Training does not authorize protected effects. Repeated requests correlate; unknown outcomes reconcile before retries.

The pure helper produces plans and negative outcomes only. It is not live transport, persistence, IAM or provider enforcement. Missing native activation is BLOCKED, not human-courier success. Runtime qualification and Operator E2E remain NOT_RUN.
